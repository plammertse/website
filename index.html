the purpose of this website

This site gives musings on things that have caught my attention over the years.

The fun part can be in applying "simple" or at least basic principles, to clarify "difficult" subjects.

Other parts are unashamedly autobiographical.

The site is really a blog, but for easier reference it is organized by subject instead of by date.

There is a book on propellers hidden in the material, and there will be a book on haptics as well.

Feel free to use any of this material, but do so at your own risk...

Some of it is tested in practice, but none of it is peer reviewed.

--  Piet Lammertse  --
