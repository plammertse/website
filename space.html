Space

From 1988 to 1998 I worked for Fokker Space, first at Schiphol, later in Leiden. I was hired to do the control simulations for the ISO satellite, and I spent the best part of five years doing that. I then graduated to the SAX satellite and a few smaller control projects.

Officially I spent the last five years in the zero-G department, which was a fun playground for someone more interested in flying than in space.

The bar above lists the most interesting projects by subject.



P. Lammertse, 2025
