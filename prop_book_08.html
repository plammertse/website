ROTATION

8   Rotational power and efficiency

8.1   Kinetic energy in the wake

Compared to the axial actuator disk, the blade elements in figure 7.2 leave behind an extra velocity u at right angles to the axial inducton v. This leaves extra kinetic energy in the wake which shows up as a rotational power loss.

By (7.4), the relative increase in kinetic energy due to the rotation at the prop disk ( but the ratio is the same in the far wake ) :

u 2 + v 2 v 2 = 1 + tan 2 φ = 1 cos 2 φ (8.1)

The last step is via trigonometry, or by (2.8). We can also arrive at the 1/cos2ϕ term directly from geometry.
