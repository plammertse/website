The Piaggio Avanti

This page TBW.


Pusher engines, tail and canard.

Two mistakes in one airplane, both due to unscientific, wishful thinking.

Assumed lower trim drag for canard airplanes.

Assumed better efficiency of pusher propellers.

I live near an airport where one of these is based. If I hear a nasty siren coming fron a speck in the sky, I know it is the Avanti passing overhead. Assumed better efficiency of pusher propellers.



P. Lammertse, 2025
