Math

I hate math

I have a tenuous relationship with math. Or maybe I should come out and say it : I hate math.

prime numbers

First of all, I really hate the prime numbers, for the same un­predictability that fascinates the true mathematician.

I see no point in riddles like Fermat's last theorem, anymore than I would ever let myself be locked in an escape room, or try to solve a crossword puzzle.

mathematical manipulations

I am terrible at rote calculation : I cannot copy a single line of math without making an error. Manipulating and simpli­fying equations is a real pain for me. I have no sense of direction in trigonometrical manipulations, and after many random steps I sometimes end up at the starting point ( if I am lucky 

  This is a problem for someone who loves physics, because physics and mathematics go hand in hand. But that is applied mathematics, the continuous kind with the nice smooth real numbers, not the jumpy kind with the natural numbers.

geometrical or algebraic

Mathematicians come in two flavors : the ones who do abstract logical manipulations in their head, and the ones with a geometrical mindset. Even though I am not a mathematician, I am firmly in the latter group. I can only accept a result if I can "see" it in a more or less geometrical way.

  I do not see my way forward at all in purely abstract reasoning. But once I have found a more or less geo­metric­al view, I believe it gives me a good bullshit detector. I will not easily be fooled into a false sense of security about a fallacy.

what I hope to achieve

This is a long winded introduction to a short section on math. I hope to make life easier for those similarly in­clined. What I offer is a geometrical intuition on a number of subjects which are often perceived as im­penetrable.

  With such tools in hand it suddenly becomes fun to understand, and do confident sanity checking on, all sorts of numerical relations, physical or otherwise.



P. Lammertse, 2025
