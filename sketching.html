Sketching

- refer to prototyping ("Sketching User Experiences").

hand sketching

To me, the only way to think about design or structure, is to sketch my ideas on paper.

It is also the only way I know of to convey my ideas to someone else.

This is why I categorize sketching more as a way of documenting ideas, than as a design tool.

But I guess it is both. Just like ideas form as you are writing them up in text, designs form while you are drawing them by hand.

- for me this does not happen when drawing in a CAD package on a screen.

  maybe I never became fluent enough in that language, but eith the present state of the art there is evidnce that children learn to draw by their motor skills while handloing a pen, not just by "willing" a picture on a screen.
