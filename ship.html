Ships

if only it had wings

Ships were never more than a passing interest to me. I never liked water. For some reason, I like airplanes.
But if a ship has a wing, it can become interesting. I dabbled briefly in hydrofoils, and in sail wing design, and I had a look at whale tail propulsion.

or a propeller

Ships are more familiar to me in their propulsion. In addition to sailwings, whale tails, and rotor wings, my work on aircraft propellers also applies one-on-one to ship propellers. The tab above adds some extra considerations on ship propellers.
