Ships

Ships were never more than a passing interest to me. I never liked water. For some reason, I like airplanes.

But if a ship has a wing, it can become interesting. I dabbled briefly in hydrofoils , and in sailing.

By the way, my work on propellers also applies one-on-one to ships.



P. Lammertse, 2025
