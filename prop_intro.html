Propellers

book

This website contains a book on propeller theory, see the tab on the menu.

I put my take on the state of the art in classical propeller theory in a paperback ready for printing, but then I decided to put the full text on line here, for easier and free access. The printed version is also available,
just drop me a line.

Below is a list of applications that sparked my interest over the years.

rubber powered models and Delft

Ever since I tried to fly rubber powered models as a kid, I have had a keen interest in propellers.

In Delft, I followed the mesmerizing lectures by prof. Dobbinga on the subject. In retrospect, they were good Dutch translations and re-interprations of Glauert's classic treatment in Durand. Sadly, in the Delft curriculum it was not the habit to let students use the original literature.

I tried to apply Dobbinga's theory to my models, but frankly they never performed the way I hoped.

human powered flight

Much later, I found articles by Eugene Larrabee on the vast increase in performance of a paddle-shaped pro­pel­ler over straight blades in the "Gossamer Condor" man-powered aircraft. It made the difference between a flight of 1 mile maximum and a flight from Crete to Santorini.

computer programs

I tried my hand at implementing classical propeller design in what turned out to be Adkins' version of Larrabee's version of Glauert's version of Betz's method as given in Durand, first on Texas TI-58 calculators, then on a Sharp MZ-700 MSX home computer, and finally on PC's in Matlab. There it stopped for a while.

electric flight

Recently, my interest was rekindled by a project at InHolland University in Delft to electrify a Dragonfly canard aircraft. This gave me the push to try my hand at writing a textbook on propeller theory, and this in turn led to revisiting the idea of optimizing the viscous loss along with the other losses which Glauert had solved only partly, and which I believe has since been mostly ignored. It became a chapter in the book which you will find on this website.
