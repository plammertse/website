Website

The website you are looking at is, if you like, an extract of the contents of my memo system. In other words, it is a certain view of my personal "mind map".

  I had wanted to do a website like this for a long time, but I did not know how. My main problem was in hosting. I believe this was more expensive and more volatile in the past than it is today. Another potential problem was IP, or  maybe even just differences of opinion, with my employers.

  Technically, I knew how to make a simple website. In fact, I had already made an interactive website and server for the "web" user interface of the Haptic Master. Its pages were built by the realtime software in plain HTML and CSS, responding to CGI-type commands from the browser.

  It was only recently that I found out that it has become commonplace to make your own website with companies like WordPress. When I looked at their themes and templates however, it seemed to me that they were too fancy to my old-fashioned liking. I therefore decided to hack together a simple website almost directly in HTML and CSS, using examples from W3Schools, and host it in github's "Pages".

 You are looking at the result. It is, as always, a work in progress. Some architectural choices were made because I do not know better. I have tried to avoid all scripting in JavaScript or similar, and all references to outside re­sources, for better longevity. I hope it works.



P. Lammertse, 2025
